How it works

Every parcel follows the same four steps, and you can see each one.

  1. {{ icon("store") }}

    Seller registers

    The seller enters the parcel and gets a tracking number.

  2. {{ icon("user") }}

    Admin assigns

    RapidExpress gives the parcel to a rider near you.

  3. {{ icon("truck") }}

    Rider delivers

    The rider updates the status and takes a photo on delivery.

  4. {{ icon("search") }}

    You track it

    Check the status here any time with your tracking number.
